# PressBig landing (pressbig.com)

Scope: the PressBig tenant's home page and the shell, search/category and article pages that inherit its world. Visitor mode: Persuade (earn the click through to today's picks and return tomorrow).

Audience and job: broad mainstream readers, quick and pleasant read; the action is opening a story (own article in place, wire story in a new tab) or a section.
Proof/content: MediaStack wire stories (source, headline, short description, image, date) for Entertainment, Fashion & Style, Gadgets, Celebrity & Culture; no own articles at launch; nothing invented (no bylines, readers, counts of "editors").
Constraints: not hard news; one tenant inside a shared Next.js app; wire links open in a new tab and are attributed to their source; phone first.
Unresolved: logo/favicon assets; ad placements.

## Direction contract

THESIS: PressBig is the day's edit as a poster hoarding torn back by hand: today's picks are the top layer, the tear exposes the section layers beneath. Refuses the hero plus three-column card grid every lifestyle blog ships.
OWN-WORLD: Chalk white #F2EFE6 ground over paste-gray backing; poster black #1A1A1A, faded indigo #445C7A, paper red #B34A4A, wheat paste #DBC26B as the only inks. Fibrous torn paper edges, halftone images, paper grain. Heavy condensed grotesque for display, one bookish serif for contrast words, mono for captions and body. Buttons are torn paper strips. No gradients, no shadows beyond paper lift.
STORY: The visitor sees a loud, tactile, pop-culture wall and understands within a second it is a daily edit of entertainment, fashion, gadgets and culture, believes it is chosen because each section is a layer with a few pasted picks rather than a feed, and clicks the lead pick or a section layer.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left half on chalk: stacked display headline "Peel / back / today." in black, indigo, red, a mono one-line promise, two torn-strip buttons (read the lead pick, see all sections). Right half: a vertical tear from top to bottom; beyond it four torn strips stacked in black, indigo, red and wheat, each carrying a section name in giant type and linking to that section; three small pasted story scraps overlap the tear. A torn "next up" card with the lead story starts at the bottom edge.
FORM: Decollage torn hoarding, challenger design-canon-decollage-torn-billboard (user-chosen from the dealt hand); seed: a179a33151f19770
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
